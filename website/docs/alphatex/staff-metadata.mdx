This page documents the [staff metadata](./syntax#staff-metadata) scope — the [metadata tags](./syntax#metadata-tag) that configure a single staff, such as its instrument, tuning, and articulation table.

Set these tags on the first bar of the staff to avoid unexpected side effects further in.

Some entries here remain metadata tags for historical reasons. They may move to staff properties in a future release.
