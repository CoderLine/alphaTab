alphaTab ships a full playback stack — a TinySoundFont-based synthesizer, a per-track mixer, a metronome and count-in, and hooks into the host platform's audio backend. On top of that, sync points let you drive the cursor from an external backing track, a video, or a MIDI-out pipeline.

Every capability below is available on Web, .NET, and Android unless a card names specific platforms. Follow any card into the [reference](/docs/reference/api/) for the exact signature.
