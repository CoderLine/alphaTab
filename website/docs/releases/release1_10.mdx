## 1.10.0

### Music Notation

#### render: transposed tabs on playable strings

Guitar tabs of a track transposed with [`notation.transpositionPitches`](/docs/reference/settings/notation/transpositionpitches) (or the alphaTex `\transpose` metadata tag) no longer show negative frets or frets beyond the neck. A note keeps its string while the shifted fret is playable there; otherwise it moves to another string, together with the notes it is tied, slid, or hammered to. Natural harmonics move to the node of the transposed pitch. Notes that fit on no string keep their string and show the fret in the new [`display.resources.tabUnplayableFretColor`](/docs/reference/types/renderingresources/).

<AlphaTab
  tex={true}
  settings={{ notation: { transpositionPitches: [-4] } }}
>{`
\\track "Guitar"
  \\staff{score tabs}
  :4 0.6 3.5 2.4 0.3 | (0.1 0.2 0.3) 3.5 3.5 3.5 | 3.5{t} 2.3{h} 4.3 4.3{b (0 4)}
`}</AlphaTab>

The loaded score is not changed: the new positions exist only on the rendered tab. Details: [Transposed tablature](/docs/features/render/guitar-tablature#transposed-tablature).

#### render: chords and multiple voices on numbered staves
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/issues/2437

Numbered staves write every note instead of a single digit per beat. A chord is a stack of digits with the lowest note at the bottom, each with its own octave dots, accidental and augmentation dot; the duration lines stay with the lowest digit. Every voice is written on its own row, the first voice on top, with beats lined up in time.

<AlphaTab tex={true}>{`
\\track "Piano"
  \\staff {score numbered}
  \\voice
    :4 (E4 G4 C5) (F4 A4 C5) (G4 B4 D5) (E4 G4 C5) | :2 (D4 F4 B4) (C4 E4 C5)
  \\voice
    :8 C3 G3 E4 G3 F3 C4 A3 C4 | :4 G2 G3 :2 C3
`}</AlphaTab>

The previous display with a single digit of the first voice, as in Guitar Pro, is available as the new [`NumberedNoteMode.SingleNote`](/docs/reference/types/model/numberednotemode); it now shows the highest note of a chord. Details: [Chords and multiple voices](/docs/features/render/numbered-notation#chords-and-multiple-voices).

#### render: lyrics above or below the staff, or hidden
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/issues/2776

The new [`score.stylesheet.lyricsPlacement`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#lyricsplacement) places lyrics [`LyricsPlacement.Below`](/docs/reference/types/model/lyricsplacement) (default) or `LyricsPlacement.Above` the standard notation staff, or hides them with `LyricsPlacement.Hidden`, like the lyrics setting of the Guitar Pro stylesheet. Without standard notation, the lyrics move to the next shown staff (tablature, slash, numbered). alphaTex sets it with `\lyricsPlacement`, the Guitar Pro importer and exporter read and write it, and the MusicXML importer sets `Above` when all lyrics are placed above the staff. Details: [Lyrics placement](/docs/features/render/coverage-lyrics#placement).

<AlphaTab tex={true}>{`
\\lyricsPlacement above
\\track "Guitar"
\\staff {score tabs}
\\lyrics "Bend it up and let it ring"
:4 7.3{b (0 4)} 9.3 :8 7.3 9.3 :4 7.2{v} | :2 9.3{b (0 4 0)} 7.3
`}</AlphaTab>

#### render: sharp and flat tunings
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/issues/2802

Tunings keep the sharp or flat spelling of the source file per string, and the score tuning and the staff tuning draw the accidentals as music symbols. alphaTex keeps the spelling written in `\tuning` and writes it back on export, and the MusicXML importer reads it from `tuning-step` and `tuning-alter`. The new [`Tuning.spellings`](/docs/reference/types/model/tuning/#spellings) holds the written pitch of every string as a [`SpelledPitch`](/docs/reference/types/model/spelledpitch). Details: [Sharp and flat tunings](/docs/features/render/tuning-display#sharp-and-flat-tunings).

<AlphaTab tex={true}>{`
\\track "Guitar"
\\staff {tabs}
\\tuning (D#4 Bb3 Gb3 C#3 Ab2 Eb2)
:4 0.6 2.5 2.4 0.3 | 0.2 2.3 0.1 r
`}</AlphaTab>

### Kotlin

#### kotlin: platform-independent alphaTab-core library
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/issues/2458

alphaTab for Kotlin is published as two packages, like the .NET version. The new [`net.alphatab:alphaTab-core`](https://central.sonatype.com/artifact/net.alphatab/alphaTab-core) runs on any JVM 17 or newer: load files, render to PNG or SVG through alphaSkia, generate MIDI and synthesize audio on servers, in command-line tools or in desktop applications. [`net.alphatab:alphaTab`](https://central.sonatype.com/artifact/net.alphatab/alphaTab) stays the Android library with the `AlphaTabView` and is built on top of the core. Details: [Platforms and packages](/docs/platforms) and [Render on the JVM](/docs/getting-started/installation-kotlin#render-on-the-jvm).

### Upgrade Notes

#### Visual changes

* Low octave dots on numbered staves sit below the duration lines instead of between digit and lines, as in printed jiǎnpǔ.
* Numbered staves show all notes of chords and all voices. Set `notes` of the numbered staff configuration to `NumberedNoteMode.SingleNote` (alphaTex: `\defaultStaffDisplay numbered { notes singleNote }`) for one digit per beat; see [Single note mode](/docs/features/render/numbered-notation#single-note-mode).
* Tuning names show the accidental as a music symbol (`E♭`) instead of the letter `b` (`Eb`).
* Lyrics of tab-only tracks are shown below the tab staff instead of above it. With standard notation and tabs, they sit directly below the standard notation staff. Set [`score.stylesheet.lyricsPlacement`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#lyricsplacement) to `LyricsPlacement.Above` (alphaTex: `\lyricsPlacement above`) to show them above the staff.

#### Deprecated APIs

* `Tuning.noteNames`, `Tuning.getTextForTuning` and `Tuning.getTextPartsForTuning` are deprecated and keep their behavior. Use [`Tuning.getSpelling`](/docs/reference/types/model/tuning/#getspelling) and [`SpelledPitch`](/docs/reference/types/model/spelledpitch) for the written name of a string.

#### Kotlin packages

* Android apps need no change: `net.alphatab:alphaTab` brings in `net.alphatab:alphaTab-core` in the same version. Builds that do not resolve transitive dependencies must add `alphaTab-core` themselves.
* The Android installation page moved to [Installation (Kotlin)](/docs/getting-started/installation-kotlin); the old address redirects there.
