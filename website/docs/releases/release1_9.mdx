## 1.9.0

This release gives the alphaTab attribution a smaller, quieter place in the score, renders and resizes in about half the time and memory, reworks note spacing and the placement of annotations, adds per-staff control over clefs, key and time signatures and bar numbers, plays repeats and jumps in the right order, imports MusicXML files more faithfully, and loads alphaTab's core once for the page, its workers and its Audio Worklet with webpack, Rspack, Vite and Turbopack. Before the release we reviewed all of these changes again and fixed what the [review](#release-hardening) found. The website is rebuilt and now lives in the alphaTab repository. The [upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes) at the end list the visual changes, changed defaults and deprecations.

### Attribution

#### render: a smaller, quieter attribution

The "rendered by alphaTab" line below the score is replaced by a small "♪ alphaTab.net" above the top staff at the end of the first system. It is drawn in the staff line color and adds no extra line. Songs with up to 10 bars and at most 2 tracks show no attribution at all.

<AlphaTab tex={true} player={false}>{`
:8 C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5 | C5 B4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4 C4 | :4 E4 G4 C5 E5 | :2 (C4 E4 G4) (C5 E5 G5) |
:8 G5 F5 E5 D5 C5 B4 A4 G4 | :1 C4 |
:8 C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5 | C5 B4 A4 G4 F4 E4 D4 C4 | :4 E4 G4 C5 E5 | :2 (C4 E4 G4) (C5 E5 G5) |
:8 G5 F5 E5 D5 C5 B4 A4 G4 | :1 C4
`}</AlphaTab>

Many projects hid the attribution without our consent and without crediting alphaTab elsewhere. The attribution is now part of how the score is drawn and much harder to remove; there is no setting to turn it off. If you have permission for a different credit and the new placement does not fit, [get in touch](/docs/attribution#request).

The new [Attribution](/docs/attribution) page explains [why we ask for credit](/docs/attribution#why), which [alternative credits](/docs/attribution#alternatives) work, and offers [logos and badges](/docs/attribution#logos) to download.

#### license: planned move to CPAL-1.0 or similar

A future release will move from MPL-2.0 to an open-source license with an attribution clause. The choice is not settled yet; [CPAL-1.0](https://opensource.org/license/cpal_1-0) is the best fit we see so far. alphaTab stays open source and free, also for commercial use, and releases under MPL-2.0, including this one, stay under MPL-2.0. See [what changes](/docs/attribution#license-cpal).

### Performance

#### render: faster layout, and resizes that match a fresh render
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2751

The page and parchment layouts measure each bar once, decide the system breaks before they fill the systems, and reuse the systems on a resize. One rule decides which bars fit a system, for the first render, resizes and partial updates alike. A resize now gives the same result as a fresh render at that width: before, 40 % of our test cases differed, with forced line breaks one bar early, bar widths off by up to 0.5 px and shifted system heights.

Compared with 1.8.4, the first render of a real song takes 28–36 % less time, a resize 13–39 % less, and both allocate about half the memory. On a dense score (216 bars, 20 staves), the rework alone brings:

| | before the rework | 1.9.0 | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| First render (layout and paint, SVG in Node.js) | 200 ms | 105 ms | −47 % |
| Resize 1300 → 800 px / 800 → 1300 px | 63 / 55 ms | 24 / 21 ms | −63 % / −60 % |
| Memory allocated per first render | 233 MB | 114 MB | −51 % |
| Memory allocated per resize | 58 / 48 MB | 19 / 12 MB | −67 % / −74 % |
| Heap kept by a finished renderer | 152 MB | 53 MB | −65 % |
| Browser renderer memory after scrolling through the score | 410 MB | 256 MB | −38 % |
| Worker heap in the browser | 81–91 MB | 40–44 MB | about −50 % |
| Time until the visible part is ready, load / resize | 777 / 243 ms | 541 / 108 ms | −30 % / −56 % |

Full demo and all options: [Responsive resize](/docs/features/render/responsive-resize).

#### browser: less work between the page, the worker and the player
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997

* The score goes to the render worker as one transferred MessagePack buffer instead of a structured-cloned object tree, and the bounds lookup comes back as a transferred `Float64Array`.
* With the render worker, resizes are coalesced while one is in progress: during a window drag, intermediate widths raise no render events (see [`IScoreRenderer.resizeRender`](/docs/reference/types/rendering/iscorerenderer/#resizerender)).
* Parts of the score far out of view release their DOM and render again when they scroll back into view. Before, the whole rendered sheet stayed in memory after scrolling through it once. Beat highlights survive this re-render.
* The MIDI is generated once per score load and player change instead of up to four times, and with a worker the score is sent for rendering before its MIDI is generated.
* The Audio Worklet reports the played position every 10 ms instead of every 128-frame render quantum, which saves messages between the audio thread, the worker and the page. [`player.positionUpdateIntervalInMilliseconds`](/docs/reference/settings/player/positionupdateintervalinmilliseconds) sets the interval; `0` restores the previous behavior.
* [`destroy()`](/docs/reference/api/destroy) releases the loaded score.

### Music Notation

#### render: power-law note spacing and last-system justification
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2749

alphaTab spaces notes with a power-law model, the approach Dorico, MuseScore and Finale use: doubling a duration multiplies its width by [`display.spacingRatio`](/docs/reference/settings/display/spacingratio). The default is `Math.SQRT2` (about 1.414, as in Dorico); values are clamped to 1.2–2.0. Whole notes and rest bars follow the same ratio, so how much of a system they take depends on `spacingRatio`. [`display.stretchForce`](/docs/reference/settings/display/stretchforce) still sets the overall density.

The same four bars with `spacingRatio` 1.2 (first) and the default (second):

<AlphaTab tex={true} settings={{ display: { spacingRatio: 1.2 } }}>{`
\\hideDynamics
:1 C4 | :2 C4 C4 | :4 C4 C4 C4 C4 | :8 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4
`}</AlphaTab>

<AlphaTab tex={true}>{`
\\hideDynamics
:1 C4 | :2 C4 C4 | :4 C4 C4 C4 C4 | :8 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4 C4
`}</AlphaTab>

[`display.lastSystemFillThreshold`](/docs/reference/settings/display/lastsystemfillthreshold) decides when the last system stretches to the full width: once its natural width reaches the given fraction of the available width. `1` (default) never stretches it, `0` always does, and values between `0.4` and `0.7` behave like Dorico and MuseScore. It replaces [`display.justifyLastSystem`](/docs/reference/settings/display/justifylastsystem).

Full demo and all options: [Density and spacing](/docs/features/render/density-and-spacing).

Thanks to [@AvaTheArchitect](https://github.com/AvaTheArchitect) for the discussion that led to this.

#### render: effect placement against the notation
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2750

Texts, chord names, dynamics, palm-mute and let-ring brackets and the other annotations are placed against the outline of the notation (the skyline) instead of in rows spanning the whole system. An annotation moves away from the staff only where a note, stem, beam or another annotation is in the way. Charts with many annotations need less vertical space.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\track "Guitar"
\\staff {score tabs}
:8 (0.5 2.4){ch "Am" dy mf} 2.3{lr} 1.2{lr} 0.1{lr} 1.2 2.3{txt "rit."} :4 2.4 |
:8 0.6{pm} 0.6{pm} 3.6{pm} 0.6{pm} :16 12.1{txt "solo" h} 15.1{h} 12.1 14.2 :8 17.1{v} 12.1
`}</AlphaTexSample>

`Am` and `rit.` sit right above the low notes of the first bar, while the high sixteenth notes in the second bar push the labels and `solo` up. The placement is always active. [`display.effectBandPaddingBottom`](/docs/reference/settings/display/effectbandpaddingbottom) sets the gap between an annotation row and the staff or the row below it.

Full demo and all options: [Effect placement](/docs/features/render/effect-placement#skyline).

#### notation: per-staff display of clefs, key and time signatures, bar numbers and tab rests
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2752
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2946

Each staff type (standard notation, tablature, slash and numbered notation) has its own configuration for the elements it can draw: clef, key signature, time signature, bar numbers and, on tablature, rests. Each element can be hidden, drawn only on the topmost staff type of a staff, or drawn only in the first system. You set this score-wide on the stylesheet ([`RenderStylesheet.scoreConfig`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#scoreconfig) and its siblings), per staff ([`Staff.scoreConfig`](/docs/reference/types/model/staff/#scoreconfig)) or per bar ([`Bar.scoreDisplay`](/docs/reference/types/model/bar/#scoredisplay)). The values are [`ElementDisplay`](/docs/reference/types/model/elementdisplay) records.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\staff {score tabs}
\\staffDisplay tabs { clefVisibility false tsPlacement allStaves restsPlacement allStaves }
:4 3.3 r 0.2 1.2 | 3.2 1.2 r 0.2
`}</AlphaTexSample>

The tab staff drops its TAB clef and repeats the time signature and the rests, which alphaTab otherwise draws only on the standard notation staff above it. The defaults render as before, apart from bar numbers (see the next item).

Full demo and all options: [Staff element display](/docs/features/render/staff-element-display#score-staff-and-bar-level).

#### notation: bar numbering with pick-up bars, custom numbers and texts
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2907

Bar numbers belong to the master bar. Pick-up bars get no number and do not count. [`MasterBar.customBarNumber`](/docs/reference/types/model/masterbar/#custombarnumber) restarts the count, and [`MasterBar.customBarNumberText`](/docs/reference/types/model/masterbar/#custombarnumbertext) shows a text such as `1a` instead of the number. In alphaTex, [`\barNumber`](/docs/alphatex/bar-metadata#barnumber) sets either one.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\defaultBarNumberDisplay allBars
\\ac :4 G4 |
:2 C5 E5 |
\\barNumber "1a" :2 D5 F5 |
\\barNumber 5 :1 C5
`}</AlphaTexSample>

The pick-up bar has no number, the third bar shows `1a`, and the last bar restarts the count at 5. By default, alphaTab now numbers only the first bar of each system. The number is centered above the start barline and no longer adds space at the start of the bar. Clef, key signature and time signature line up across the staves of a system. The MusicXML importer reads the `number` attribute of each measure.

Full demo and all options: [Bar numbers](/docs/features/render/bar-numbers#display-modes).

#### notation: tuning on the staff lines
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2742

alphaTab can write the note name of each open string at the left end of its tab line on the first system, instead of the tuning block above the score. This matches the "tuning before staff" option in Guitar Pro. Select it with [`RenderStylesheet.tuningDisplayMode`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#tuningdisplaymode) set to `TuningDisplayMode.Staff`, or with [`\tuningDisplayMode staff`](/docs/alphatex/score-metadata#tuningdisplaymode) in alphaTex. [`display.inlineTuningPaddingRight`](/docs/reference/settings/display/inlinetuningpaddingright) sets the gap between the labels and the staff.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\tuningDisplayMode staff
\\track "Guitar"
\\staff { tabs }
\\tuning (E4 B3 G3 D3 A2 D2)
:8 0.6 2.4 0.3 2.2 0.1 2.2 0.3 2.4
`}</AlphaTexSample>

Guitar Pro 7 and 8 files store the mode in their stylesheet; alphaTab reads it and the Guitar Pro 7 exporter writes it.

Full demo and all options: [Tuning display](/docs/features/render/tuning-display#tuning-on-staff).

Thanks to [@kulek1](https://github.com/kulek1) for contributing this feature, and to [@AvaTheArchitect](https://github.com/AvaTheArchitect) for the discussion that led to it.

#### notation: drum tablature
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2591

Percussion staves can render as a drum tablature, as in Guitar Pro and TuxGuitar. Each drum piece sits on a line, and the number on the line is its articulation number. Turn it on with [`Staff.showTablature`](/docs/reference/types/model/staff/#showtablature), or with `\staff { score tabs }` in alphaTex. In alphaTex, `@` assigns the line of a percussion note: `SnareHit@3` places the snare on line 3.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\track "Drums" { instrument percussion }
\\staff { score tabs }
\\clef neutral
\\articulation defaults
:4 (KickHit@5 HiHatClosed@1) (SnareHit@3 HiHatClosed@1) (KickHit@5 HiHatClosed@1) (SnareHit@3 HiHatClosed@1)
`}</AlphaTexSample>

Guitar Pro 7 and 8 files keep the line of every drum note on import and export.

Full demo and all options: [Drum and percussion notation](/docs/features/render/drum-tabs#drum-tablature).

Thanks to [@leocaseiro](https://github.com/leocaseiro) for contributing this feature, and to [@sugizo](https://github.com/sugizo) for the discussion that led to it.

#### notation: hammer-on, pull-off and slide labels above the staff
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2945
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2943

The `H`, `P` and `sl.` labels introduced in 1.8.3 are drawn above the staff instead of on the slur arcs, on standard notation, tablature and numbered notation. Each beat gets one label, centered on its slur segment; different labels on one beat stack. A segment across a system break is labeled on the first system only. Regular slurs carry no label.

Slurs between chords follow Behind Bars and Guitar Pro: on standard notation one slur joins the outer noteheads of the chord, on tablature each beat gets at most one arc above and one below. On narrow screens these slurs no longer disappear.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\staff {score tabs}
:4 r r 5.3{h} 7.3{h} | 5.3 r r (5.1{h} 5.2{sl}) | (7.1 9.2) r r r
`}</AlphaTexSample>

Hide the labels with [`NotationElement.EffectHammerOnPullOffText`](/docs/reference/types/notationelement#effecthammeronpullofftext) and [`NotationElement.EffectSlideText`](/docs/reference/types/notationelement#effectslidetext) in [`notation.elements`](/docs/reference/settings/notation/elements).

Full demo and all options: [Effects and articulations](/docs/features/render/coverage-effects-articulations#slide-labels).

Thanks to [@shimabox](https://github.com/shimabox) for reporting the problem with slurs between chords.

#### notation: centered full-bar notes and rests
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2781

When a bar holds a single beat that lasts the whole bar, alphaTab centers it in the bar together with its annotations, following Behind Bars. This applies when every staff and voice rendered in the bar has one such beat; other bars keep the regular positioning.

#### notation: rest position per beat
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2721

A rest can take the staff position of a given pitch instead of the automatic one. In alphaTex, write the beat property [`restDisplayPitch`](/docs/alphatex/beat-properties#restdisplaypitch); in the model, set [`Beat.restDisplayTone`](/docs/reference/types/model/beat/#restdisplaytone) and [`Beat.restDisplayOctave`](/docs/reference/types/model/beat/#restdisplayoctave). The position follows the clef and works per voice.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\hideDynamics
:4 r C5 r C5 | r{restDisplayPitch A5} C5 r{restDisplayPitch E4} C5
`}</AlphaTexSample>

The first bar shows the automatic positions, the second bar moves the rests to A5 and E4. alphaTab does not read rest positions from Guitar Pro or MusicXML files, and the alphaTex exporter does not write `restDisplayPitch`.

Full demo and all options: [Rests and full-bar notes](/docs/features/render/coverage-rests#rest-position).

### Player

#### player: repeats, alternate endings and jumps play in the right order
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2915
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2895
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2917
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2929

The player, the cursor and MIDI export follow one set of rules for repeats and jumps:

* Each D.C. and D.S. is taken once. Before, a coda placed before the bar that jumps back could make playback loop forever.
* After a jump, repeats play as their final pass: the passage plays once, with only its last alternate ending, as in MuseScore.
* Repeat passes are counted per repeat group, so with Guitar Pro style repeat counts a repeat sign in several alternate endings plays every ending once, as in Guitar Pro 8. MusicXML files whose endings each close with a backward repeat but give no `times` can play fewer endings.
* A jump inside a repeat, or on its closing bar, is taken after all repeat passes.
* A jump on an alternate ending that playback skips is not taken.
* MusicXML files that write jumps and markers as `<sound>` attributes inside a `<direction>`, as MuseScore exports them, play these jumps.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\track "Guitar"
\\staff {score}
\\ro :4 C4 D4 E4 F4 |
\\ae 1 \\rc 2 G4 F4 E4 D4 |
\\ae 2 \\jump fine G4 E4 :2 C4 |
\\jump daCapoAlFine :4 A4 G4 F4 E4
`}</AlphaTexSample>

This plays bars 1 2 1 3 4 1 3: after the D.C. the repeat plays as its final pass, so bar 1 continues into the second ending, where `Fine` ends the piece.

Full demo and all options: [Repeats and structural elements](/docs/features/render/coverage-repeats#playback-order).

Thanks to [@AnshulJ999](https://github.com/AnshulJ999) and [@gabbe](https://github.com/gabbe) for reporting these problems, and to [@noy-solvin](https://github.com/noy-solvin) for contributing the fix for repeats in alternate endings.

#### player: pick-up bars for metronome, count-in and beaming
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2939

alphaTab treats a pick-up bar as the end of a full bar. Beaming, triplet feel and metronome clicks follow the beats of the time signature. The count-in counts one full bar and continues up to the beat where playback starts, also when you start in the middle of a bar.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\ts (2 4)
\\ac C4.8 D4.8 E4.8 |
F4.4 G4.4 |
A4.8 B4.8 C5.8 D5.8
`}</AlphaTexSample>

The pick-up holds the last three eighths of a 2/4 bar: C keeps its own flag, and D and E are beamed as beat 2. The same change corrects beat timers with several tracks and tempo changes within a bar, and reading [`MidiFile.events`](/docs/reference/types/midi/midifile) with `MidiFileFormat.MultiTrack` no longer overflows the stack.

Full demo and all options: [Count-in and metronome](/docs/features/play-sync/count-in-and-metronome#pick-up-bars).

Thanks to [@AlbinoBJJ](https://github.com/AlbinoBJJ) for reporting this problem.

#### integration: separate settings for seek, range selection and click reset
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2779

Three settings replace [`player.enableUserInteraction`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableuserinteraction):

* [`player.enableSeekToClick`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableseektoclick) moves the playhead to the clicked beat.
* [`player.enablePlaybackRangeSelection`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableplaybackrangeselection) selects a playback range by dragging.
* [`player.resetPlaybackRangeOnClick`](/docs/reference/settings/player/resetplaybackrangeonclick) clears the range on a plain click. With `false`, a click keeps the range and seeks within it.

All three default to `true`. A drag creates a range only once it reaches another beat; a press and release on the same beat counts as a click, even if the mouse moved a little.

```js
import * as alphaTab from '@coderline/alphatab';

const api = new alphaTab.AlphaTabApi(document.getElementById('alphaTab'), {
  core: { file: '/scores/song.gp' },
  player: {
    playerMode: alphaTab.PlayerMode.EnabledAutomatic,
    enableSeekToClick: true,
    enablePlaybackRangeSelection: true,
    resetPlaybackRangeOnClick: false,
  },
});
```

Full demo and all options: [Enabling and disabling interaction](/docs/features/interact/enabling-and-disabling-interaction#keep-the-range-on-click).

Thanks to [@AvaTheArchitect](https://github.com/AvaTheArchitect) for the discussion that led to this.

### alphaTex Extensions

#### alphaTex: staff display tags
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2946

Three tags express the staff element display settings: [`\defaultStaffDisplay`](/docs/alphatex/score-metadata#defaultstaffdisplay) for the whole score, [`\staffDisplay`](/docs/alphatex/staff-metadata#staffdisplay) for the current staff and [`\barDisplay`](/docs/alphatex/bar-metadata#bardisplay) for the current bar. Each takes optional staff types (`score`, `tabs`, `slash`, `numbered`, several in parentheses) and a property list such as `clefVisibility`, `tsPlacement` or `barNumber`. The alphaTex exporter writes these settings, so they survive a round trip.

<AlphaTexSample player={true}>{`
\\staff {score tabs}
:4 3.3 r 0.2 1.2 |
\\barDisplay tabs { tsPlacement allStaves } \\ts (3 4) 3.3 0.2 r | 1.2 r 0.2
`}</AlphaTexSample>

Only the change to 3/4 is restated on the tab staff.

#### musicxml: note spelling from step and alter

Full list of supported elements: [MusicXML feature table](/docs/formats/musicxml#feature-table).

### Bundlers and Frameworks

#### bundlers: one alphaTab core for the page, the workers and the worklet
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998

With webpack, Rspack and Vite, the page, the Web Workers and the Audio Worklet load alphaTab's core from one shared chunk instead of a copy each, so the browser downloads and compiles about 1.2 MiB (minified) once instead of up to three times. alphaTab starts its worker with the standard `new Worker(new URL('./alphaTab.worker.mjs', import.meta.url), { type: 'module' })` and its worklet from `alphaTab.worklet.mjs`, both importing `alphaTab.core.mjs`.

The new page [Installing in web projects](/docs/getting-started/installation-web-projects) lists the setup for every bundler and framework with the number of core copies each one loads, and links to a verified sample project for each. New pages cover [other bundlers](/docs/getting-started/installation-other-bundlers), [React and Next.js](/docs/getting-started/installation-react), [Vue and Nuxt](/docs/getting-started/installation-vue), [Angular](/docs/getting-started/installation-angular), [Svelte and SvelteKit](/docs/getting-started/installation-svelte) and [Solid](/docs/getting-started/installation-solid).

#### webpack: Rspack and Rsbuild support
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998

`@coderline/alphatab-webpack` works with webpack 5 (5.90 or later), Rspack 1.7 and 2, and Rsbuild through `tools.rspack`. It bundles alphaTab's worker and worklet as entries and moves the core into an `alphaTabCore` split chunk. For that it sets `optimization.usedExports` to `'global'` in production builds when you did not set it. See [Installation (webpack, Rspack, Rsbuild)](/docs/getting-started/installation-webpack).

#### vite: worker and worklet as chunks of the main build
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998

`@coderline/alphatab-vite` emits alphaTab's worker and worklet as chunks of the main build, sharing the core chunk with the page, and supports Vite 6, 7 and 8. The URLs are written relative to the chunk that references them, so a relative `base`, `renderBuiltUrl`, SvelteKit and Nuxt 4 work. In the dev server, alphaTab is excluded from dependency pre-bundling. See [Installation (Vite)](/docs/getting-started/installation-vite).

#### integration: entries for bundlers without a plugin
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998

The new exports `@coderline/alphatab/core`, `@coderline/alphatab/worker` and `@coderline/alphatab/worklet` let your app own alphaTab's worker and worklet entries. `@coderline/alphatab/core` exports the full API and starts nothing on its own; you register the worker and worklet with [`Environment.initializeMain`](/docs/features/extend/worker-and-worklet-initialization). [Installation (other bundlers)](/docs/getting-started/installation-other-bundlers) shows the setup for esbuild, Rollup and Parcel.

#### bundlers: Turbopack detection
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998

alphaTab detects Turbopack ([`Environment.isTurbopackBundled`](/docs/reference/types/environment), also printed by `Environment.printEnvironmentInfo()`) and starts its workers in the form Turbopack bundles, sharing the core chunk with the page. Turbopack does not bundle Audio Worklets, so there alphaTab loads its worklet from [`core.scriptFile`](/docs/reference/settings/core/scriptfile). See [Next.js with Turbopack](/docs/getting-started/installation-react#turbopack).

#### Other changes

* bundlers: the minified files reference each other: `alphaTab.min.mjs` imports `alphaTab.core.min.mjs` and starts `alphaTab.worker.min.mjs` and `alphaTab.worklet.min.mjs`, which import the minified core too, so a CDN or copied setup loads only minified files.
* player: without a detectable script file, loading the Audio Worklet fails with the error "Could not detect alphaTab script file, cannot initialize audio worklet".
* webpack: `webWorkers: false` in the plugin options turns off the worker bundling again ([#2998](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2998)), and the build reports an error instead of hanging when the plugin cannot copy its font and SoundFont files ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997))

* bundlers: in the minified files, `Ottavia._8va` and the other enum members starting with an underscore are defined again, and enum values are inlined across modules, which saves about 10 KB gzip on the minified core ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997))

### Release hardening
https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2990

Before cutting the release, we reviewed the 1.9 changes across rendering, importers, player and packaging. Each finding is fixed in the layer that causes it and comes with a test. Most items below go back to that review; the fixes listed in the sections above are not repeated here.

**Rendering and layout**

* Bend and whammy slurs and tab bends enter the [skyline](/docs/features/render/effect-placement#skyline) with their drawn shape, so "grad.", let ring and other labels no longer sit on them. Dynamics below the staff keep clear of accents.
* Bars squeezed below their natural width take space from the widest gaps first and keep the minimum width of their content.
* Full-bar notes are centered between the bar's start (clef, key and time signature) and its barline; pick-up bars and empty voices no longer prevent the centering.
* Bars with only grace notes place them side by side, dense dynamics get horizontal room, and the ledger lines of neighbouring notes stay apart.
* Beat texts, lyrics, free-time texts and dynamics are spaced only against their own staff; texts on other tracks no longer widen the bar.
* Bar numbers show on multi-bar rests, are no longer clipped at partial boundaries in the horizontal layout, and with [`RenderStylesheet.hideEmptyStaves`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#hideemptystaves) sit on the first visible track.
* String-number circles are stacked by pitch, and adjacent separate trills are drawn as separate trills ([`Note.isTrillStart`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#istrillstart) marks where one starts).
* A two-voice unison whose note head is displaced by a second, and beams over rests only in numbered notation (for example a whole rest), render instead of failing.
* Canvas text aligned at the top sits at the same height in Firefox as in Chromium.

**Importers and model**

* Unset note values (`NaN`, see [For integrations](#for-integrations)) survive a JSON round trip, and score JSON from 1.8 with `-1` loads as unset.
* Guitar Pro 3–5: the MIDI port and channel are range-checked, a negative fret on a stringed staff becomes a dead note, and notes stored without a fret are dropped.
* Drum tablature is shown according to the file: the staff flags in Guitar Pro 5, the part configuration in Guitar Pro 6–8. Files without the flag show no drum tablature.
* Guitar Pro 6: ties survive on staves that get a generated fingering, and track pan and volume come from the RSE channel strip.
* MusicXML: `measure-numbering` holds until the file changes it, a `<string>` without `<fret>` on a tuned staff gets its fret from the pitch, and notes without a tab position get no tab glyphs.
* A custom bar number of `0` is respected.

**Player and integration**

* A custom cursor handler set while the cursors already exist is attached, and the previous handler is detached.
* Changes of [`player.scrollMode`](/docs/reference/settings/player/scrollmode) through [`updateSettings()`](/docs/reference/api/updatesettings) take effect.
* When the browser refuses to play a [backing track](/docs/features/play-sync/backing-track-audio), the player pauses instead of reporting that it plays.
* With the worker renderer, bounds resolve against the score that was actually rendered, so a render started while `load()` is still running no longer throws.
* [`exportAudio()`](/docs/reference/api/exportaudio) loads the SoundFont presets the exported song needs.

**Packaging and platforms**

* `@coderline/alphatab/vite` and `@coderline/alphatab/webpack` forward to `@coderline/alphatab-vite` and `@coderline/alphatab-webpack`, which `@coderline/alphatab` declares as optional peer dependencies.
* `@coderline/alphatab-language-server` loads with `require`, its `bin` starts with a hashbang, and it and `@coderline/alphatab-monaco` declare the `vscode-languageserver` packages they import.
* [`AlphaTexAtTokenNode`](/docs/reference/types/importer/alphatex/alphatexattokennode) is exported.
* Deprecated members are marked `[Obsolete]` in C# and `@Deprecated` in Kotlin, with their documentation.
* The settings reference documents the actual defaults of [`display.staffPaddingLeft`](/docs/reference/settings/display/staffpaddingleft) and [`display.systemsLayoutMode`](/docs/reference/settings/display/systemslayoutmode), and every API added in 1.9.0 is marked "since 1.9.0".

Restore numbers on every bar with [`\defaultBarNumberDisplay allBars`](/docs/alphatex/score-metadata#defaultbarnumberdisplay) in alphaTex, or set `barNumber` to `BarNumberDisplay.AllBars` on the four staff configurations of the stylesheet. See [Bar numbers](/docs/features/render/bar-numbers#display-modes).
* The engraving setting [`preBeatGlyphSpacing`](/docs/reference/types/engravingsettings/#prebeatglyphspacing) changes from 0.5 to 0.6 staff spaces (4.5 to 5.4 at the default size), and the new `staffContentPadding` (1 staff space) keeps the content of neighbouring staves apart ([#2907](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2907)).
* Guitar Pro 3–5 files are decoded with [`importer.gp3To5encoding`](/docs/reference/settings/importer/gp3to5encoding), `windows-1252` by default, instead of UTF-8 ([#2774](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2774)). Set it to `utf-8` for the previous decoding. A value other than `utf-8` in `importer.encoding` still takes precedence. See [Tuning importer behavior](/docs/features/import-load/tuning-importer-behavior#importerencoding-and-importergp3to5encoding).
* A drag creates a playback range only once it reaches another beat, and a click that seeks while a range stays active is clamped to the range ([#2779](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2779)). See [Enabling and disabling interaction](/docs/features/interact/enabling-and-disabling-interaction#keep-the-range-on-click).

#### Deprecations

The deprecated members still work.

* [`player.enableUserInteraction`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableuserinteraction): use [`player.enableSeekToClick`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableseektoclick), [`player.enablePlaybackRangeSelection`](/docs/reference/settings/player/enableplaybackrangeselection) and [`player.resetPlaybackRangeOnClick`](/docs/reference/settings/player/resetplaybackrangeonclick). Setting it writes all three ([#2779](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2779)). See [Migrating from `enableUserInteraction`](/docs/features/interact/enabling-and-disabling-interaction#migrating-from-enableuserinteraction).
* [`display.justifyLastSystem`](/docs/reference/settings/display/justifylastsystem): use [`display.lastSystemFillThreshold`](/docs/reference/settings/display/lastsystemfillthreshold). `true` maps to `0`, `false` to `1` ([#2749](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2749)).
* [`RenderStylesheet.barNumberDisplay`](/docs/reference/types/model/renderstylesheet/#barnumberdisplay) and [`Bar.barNumberDisplay`](/docs/reference/types/model/bar/#barnumberdisplay): use the `barNumber` field of the staff configurations and bar overrides. Setting the old fields writes all four staff types ([#2752](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2752)). See [Deprecated bar number fields](/docs/features/render/staff-element-display#deprecated-bar-number-fields).

#### For integrations

* With `@coderline/alphatab-webpack`, alphaTab's core is a chunk of its own that the page loads next to your entry script. HTML generated by `HtmlWebpackPlugin`, `HtmlRspackPlugin`, Rsbuild or Next.js includes it. A hand-written `<script>` list must include every file of the entrypoint, and hosts that only inject chunks they know by name, such as Angular's webpack builder, set `optimization.splitChunks.cacheGroups.alphaTabCore = false`. See [Optimization settings](/docs/getting-started/installation-webpack#optimization).
* `@coderline/alphatab-webpack` sets `optimization.usedExports` to `'global'` in production builds when you did not set it, and warns when it is `true`, which bundles the core twice.
* With Vite and Rsbuild, the scripts are emitted into a subfolder (`assets/`, `static/js/`), so set [`core.fontDirectory`](/docs/reference/settings/core/fontdirectory) to the font the plugin copies; see [Point alphaTab to the font and the SoundFont](/docs/getting-started/installation-web-projects#assets).
* Unset values of [`Note.fret`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#fret), [`Note.string`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#string), [`Note.octave`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#octave), [`Note.tone`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#tone) and [`Note.percussionArticulation`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#percussionarticulation) are `NaN` instead of `-1` ([#2591](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2591)). Replace `=== -1` checks with [`note.isStringed`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#isstringed), [`note.isPercussion`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#ispercussion), [`note.isPiano`](/docs/reference/types/model/note/#ispiano) or `Number.isNaN(...)`. A percussion note in a drum tablature is both percussion and stringed.
* Custom [`IMidiFileHandler`](/docs/reference/types/midi/imidifilehandler) implementations need the new member [`addMetronome(tick, counter, durationInTicks)`](/docs/reference/types/midi/imidifilehandler/#addmetronome); handlers that write standard MIDI files can ignore the call. In pick-up bars, [`Automation.ratioPosition`](/docs/reference/types/model/automation/#ratioposition) is relative to the full bar of the time signature, as Guitar Pro writes it ([#2939](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2939)).
* [`AutomationType.Bank`](/docs/reference/types/model/automationtype) changes from `4` to `5`; in 1.8 it had the same value as `AutomationType.SyncPoint`. Score JSON written by 1.8 stores bank changes as `4`, which 1.9 loads as sync points ([#2907](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2907)).
* Guitar Pro 3–5 files: each track gets the MIDI port and channel stored in the file and its own [`PlaybackInformation`](/docs/reference/types/model/playbackinformation). [`port`](/docs/reference/types/model/playbackinformation/#port) is zero-based (1.8 kept the one-based value of the file), and [`primaryChannel`](/docs/reference/types/model/playbackinformation/#primarychannel) can differ from 1.8, for example `2` instead of `4`. Programs, volume, balance and the notes do not change, and two tracks that 1.8 played on one channel get a channel each. Code that addresses MIDI channels or ports per track sees the new numbers ([#2774](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2774)).
* Settings JSON ([`RenderingResourcesJson`](/docs/reference/types/json/renderingresourcesjson)) accepts the deprecated fonts `copyrightFont`, `titleFont`, `subTitleFont`, `wordsFont`, `timerFont`, `directionsFont`, `fretboardNumberFont`, `markerFont` and `barNumberFont` and applies them as the properties of the same name do; 1.8 ignored them in JSON. `effectFont`, `fingeringFont` and `inlineFingeringFont` are still accepted and still have no effect ([#2749](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2749)).
* The SVG output is no longer identical between two renders of the same score: the staff lines of the bar with the attribution and the attribution itself are written as one path in a random order. The SVG text also has no newlines between elements, black rectangles have no `fill` attribute, and single music symbols end with `;` ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997)). The drawn result is the same. Compare rendered images instead of SVG strings.
* The page and its alphaTab worker must load the same alphaTab version: the messages between them changed (MessagePack score, flat bounds lookup) ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997)).
* With the render worker, resizes requested while one is still running are combined into one resize with the latest width. The skipped widths raise no [`renderStarted`](/docs/reference/api/renderstarted), [`renderFinished`](/docs/reference/api/renderfinished) or [`postRenderFinished`](/docs/reference/api/postrenderfinished) events ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997)).
* With the Audio Worklet, [`playerPositionChanged`](/docs/reference/api/playerpositionchanged) and [`midiEventsPlayed`](/docs/reference/api/midieventsplayed) fire at most every 10 ms by default. Set [`player.positionUpdateIntervalInMilliseconds`](/docs/reference/settings/player/positionupdateintervalinmilliseconds) to `0` for an update per render quantum ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997)).
* Internal flow messages are logged at the new [`LogLevel.Trace`](/docs/reference/types/loglevel) instead of `Debug`, and the published JavaScript files do not contain them ([#2997](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2997)).
* Fades write the expression controller (CC 11) into the generated MIDI instead of the channel volume (CC 7), and restore it with the voice's next note. MIDI files exported from alphaTab and custom [`IMidiFileHandler`](/docs/reference/types/midi/imidifilehandler) implementations see these events ([#2995](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2995)).
* Sync point times, beat timers and other millisecond values derived from ticks keep their decimals instead of being cut to whole milliseconds ([#2960](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2960)).
* C# and Kotlin: members deprecated in alphaTab carry `[Obsolete]` and `@Deprecated`, so code that uses them gets compiler warnings ([#2990](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2990)).
* alphaTex AST consumers: the values of [`AlphaTexNodeType`](/docs/reference/types/importer/alphatex/alphatexnodetype) are renumbered, so compare against the enum members instead of numbers, and `AlphaTexNoteNode.noteStringDot` is now [`noteStringSeparator`](/docs/reference/types/importer/alphatex/alphatexnotenode/#notestringseparator) of type `IAlphaTexStringSeparatorNode` ([#2591](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2591)).

#### Package changes

* The npm packages are published from GitHub Actions through npm trusted publishing and carry provenance attestations, so you can check which commit and workflow built each version (`npm audit signatures`). Releases go live only after a maintainer approves them ([#2959](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2959)).
* `@coderline/alphatab-vite/workerPlugin` is removed. Register `alphaTab()` from `@coderline/alphatab-vite`; it covers the worker and the worklet.
* `@coderline/alphatab-vite` accepts Vite `^6 || ^7 || ^8` as peer dependency.
* `@coderline/alphatab-webpack` declares `webpack` (`^5.0.0`) and `@rspack/core` (`^1.7.0 || ^2.0.0`) as optional peer dependencies; install the one your project builds with.
* `@coderline/alphatab` exports `./core`, `./worker` and `./worklet`. Bundlers that resolve package `exports` find them without configuration; Parcel needs `packageExports` enabled.
* `@coderline/alphatab-webpack` declares webpack as a peer dependency (`^5.0.0`) instead of a dependency. Install webpack in your project if it is not there yet ([#2806](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2806)).
* `@coderline/alphatab-language-server` has separate entry points for browsers and Node.js ([#2830](https://github.com/CoderLine/alphaTab/pull/2830)):
  * Import `startWebWorkerLanguageServer` from `@coderline/alphatab-language-server/server/browser` and `startNodeLanguageServer` from `@coderline/alphatab-language-server/server/node`. The package root resolves to the browser entry only for bundlers that apply the `browser` condition; TypeScript with `moduleResolution: "bundler"` does not, and reports that the root has no `startWebWorkerLanguageServer`.
  * `main`, `types` and `bin` point to `dist/index.node.*` instead of `dist/server.*`. Paths into `dist` are not exported; use the entry points above.
  * `startWebWorkerLanguageServer` takes a `LanguageServerMessagePort`: any `MessagePort`, `Worker` or worker `self` fits, and its declaration type checks with either the DOM or the WebWorker library.
* `@coderline/alphatab-monaco` exports its modules as subpaths: `@coderline/alphatab-monaco/lsp`, `/lspMonacoMappings`, `/alphatex`, `/textmate`, `/types` and `/worker`. Imports of `@coderline/alphatab-monaco/dist/<module>` keep working (see [Monaco](/docs/alphatex/monaco)).
* Major dependency updates: `vscode-languageserver` 9 to 11 in `@coderline/alphatab-language-server` (`@coderline/alphatab-monaco` now declares it too), `monaco-editor` 0.55 to 0.57 in `@coderline/alphatab-monaco`, and `magic-string` 0.30 to 1 in `@coderline/alphatab-vite`.
