## Introduction

[TablEdit](https://tabledit.com/) is a tablature and notation editor for fretted and other instruments, developed by Matthieu Leschemelle.
alphaTab reads `.tef` files saved by TablEdit 3. Files of TablEdit 1 and 2 are not supported: open them in TablEdit 3 and save them again.

**Compatibility Level:** New, tested against the MIDI and MusicXML exports of TablEdit itself.

TablEdit stores the notes on a fine time grid instead of in beats. alphaTab rebuilds beats and voices from that grid: notes of different length that sound together are placed in additional voices.
The playback order of a TablEdit file comes from its reading list. alphaTab expresses it through repeats, alternate endings and jump directions, and logs a warning when an order cannot be expressed exactly.
